> [Dell NetWorker](https://www.dell.com/support/product-details/en-us/product/networker/overview)
> is an enterprise backup and recovery software, formerly known as Legato NetWorker and EMC NetWorker.

Dell does not publish a written support policy for NetWorker, and lists support dates for each
release in its Software Release and End of Service Life file. Releases up to 19.12 are supported for
about 3 years, while 19.13 and 19.14 are supported for about 2 years. Dell states that these dates
are subject to change.
