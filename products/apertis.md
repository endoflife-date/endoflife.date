> Apertis (https://www.apertis.org/) is an open-source GNU/Linux distribution based on Debian, > designed primarily for automotive and other embedded devices,
> with security and modularity as two of its primary strengths.

At any given time, there are approximately two stable releases of Apertis, both based on the latest Debian stable and on the latest Linux LTS kernel at the release time.

Apertis product releases receive security and important bug fixes through dedicated security updates and quarterly point releases.
The standard support period is seven quarters, or approximately two years, with no new features normally added after the initial product release.

A [commercial support](https://www.apertis.org/overview/commercial-support/) is available to adapt Apertis to users' requirements.
