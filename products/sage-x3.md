> [Sage X3](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/products/sage-x3/) is enterprise
> resource planning software for finance, distribution, and manufacturing.

This page tracks the on-premises Sage X3 Version 12 semiannual releases.
Sage describes a 24-month maintenance period: six months of Current
maintenance, twelve months of Standard maintenance, and six months of
Extended maintenance.

Release dates come from the [Sage X3 patch list][patches]. End dates
represent the estimated end of maintenance, calculated by adding
24 months to those release dates. Customers with a valid support plan
may still have access to certain support services after maintenance ends.

[patches]: https://fr-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewsolution.jsp?solutionid=210905150097071
