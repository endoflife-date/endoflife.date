> [Cisco Business 350 Series Managed Switches](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/index.html)
> (CBS350) are managed Ethernet switches for small and medium businesses. All models run the same
> CBS350 firmware.

Cisco has announced the end of sale and end of life of the whole series in
[four notices](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/eos-eol-notice-listing.html),
following its [End-of-Life Policy](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/eos-eol-policy.html):

- [EOL15391](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/business-cbs350-series-managed-switch-mod-eol.html) for 8 models,
- [EOL15389](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/business-350-series-switches-mod-eol.html) for the other 30 models,
- [EOL15511](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/business-350-cbs350-series-managed-switches-select-models-eol.html) for the part numbers ending in `-BR` of the second group, with the same dates,
- [EOL15597](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/switches/business-350-series-managed-switches/business-350-series-managed-switches-eol.html) for the part numbers ending in `-IN` of the second group.

The dates in the table are those of the first two notices. Part numbers ending in `-IN` that fall
under EOL15597 have later dates: end of sale on 2025-01-31, end of
software maintenance and of security support on 2026-01-31
and last date of support on 2029-10-31.

The columns follow Cisco's milestones:

- **End of Sale**: the last date to order the product.
- **Software Maintenance**: the last date Cisco may release software maintenance releases or bug fixes.
- **Security Support**: the last date Cisco may release fixes for vulnerabilities or security issues.
- **Hardware Support**: the last date to receive service and support for the product.

The `show inventory` command prints the part number of a switch (the PID), including the regional
suffix such as `-EU` or `-NA`.
